A closer look

From the first pour to the last bean.

Brew stage by stage

Recipes are a list of stages: the bloom, each pour and, on a Hario Switch, when to close and open the valve. The timer walks you through them with the weight to pour to, how long is left and what comes next.

Every bag at a glance

Photograph the bag, then see how much is left, what you paid and where it is on its freshness curve. Rate it out of ten once you know it. Used-up bags move to the archive, ready to compare with the next one.

Remember how each cup tasted

Rate the cup, score acidity, sweetness, body and bitterness, add a photo and a note. Got the grind or a pour time wrong? Fix the brew afterwards, and brew it again in one tap.

Learn where it comes from

See the coffee belt on a world map, how altitude shapes flavour, and what each process does to the cup, with photos and sources for every section.

How it's built

Quiet by design.

Yours, on your device

Your beans, brews and notes are stored on your device. Coffrack works fully offline, with no ads and no tracking.

Sign in only if you want to

An account is optional. Sign in with Google if you want sync across iPhone, Android and Mac; signing in never sends your log anywhere until you switch sync on.

Calm, not busy

No streaks, badges or notifications that nag. Just a clear view of what you're brewing and how it tastes.
